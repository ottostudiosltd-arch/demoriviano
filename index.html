COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know

Curious about 3D printing, timelines, or caring for your piece? We're here to help.

How do I order a custom couple figurine or portrait design? ▾

It's simple! Fill out our custom inquiry form below or send us a message on Instagram with your reference photo. We evaluate the pose, suggest scale and styling, generate a digital 3D preview for your confirmation, and start printing once you give the green light.

What materials do you use, and are they safe and durable? ▾

We primarily use premium quality PLA (Polylactic Acid) biopolymers derived from renewable cornstarch, making our pieces eco-friendly, odorless, non-toxic, and surprisingly durable for decorative display. For intricate jewelry and miniatures, we use high-resolution photo-cured resin.

How long does fabrication and shipping take? ▾

Standard ready designs (frames, keychains, candles) take 1–3 business days. Complex custom pieces like figurines take 4–7 business days to model, print, and hand-finish. Domestic express shipping typically arrives in 2–4 days. Rush orders can be accommodated upon request.

Can I customize the color, inscription, and dimensions? ▾

Yes! We offer a wide palette of shades including matte pastels, silk metallic gold/copper, deep marble tones, and classic monochrome. Names, special dates, Roman numerals, or short messages can be seamlessly embossed or engraved into the design base.

How should I clean and care for my 3D decor pieces? ▾

Keep your pieces away from extreme high-temperature environments (such as direct boiling water or enclosed hot car dashboards exceeding 55°C). Dust gently with a soft microfiber cloth or a soft-bristled brush. They can be lightly wiped with a damp cloth if needed.
